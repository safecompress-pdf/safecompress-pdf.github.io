Get to know the extension ↗
From file to finished

Four steps. All on your device.

Install the Chrome extension, then open it from your browser’s extensions menu.

  1. Choose your PDF

    Select a scanned or image-heavy PDF from your computer.

  2. Pick a level

    Start with Medium. Try High for a smaller image resolution.

  3. Let it process

    The extension renders each page and builds a new PDF locally.

  4. Save your copy

    Download the result, compare file sizes and keep your original.

A quick heads-up: pages become images. Searchable text, clickable links and editable forms are not preserved. Read the full guide.

Visit the full FAQ ↗
Does SafeCompress upload my files?

No. The extension processes PDFs locally in your browser, without sending them to a compression server. File names and sizes can be stored in the on-device history log.

Is the extension free to use?

Yes. SafeCompress PDF is free to install and use, and no account is required.

Why can a compressed PDF become larger?

The extension rebuilds pages as images. Text-heavy and already-optimized PDFs can therefore become larger. It works best with scanned and image-heavy documents.

Will my text still be searchable?

No. Rasterization replaces the original text layer with page images. Text selection, clickable links and editable forms are not preserved. Always keep your original file.
