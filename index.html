How it works

  1. Choose PDF — PDF-only picker accept="application/pdf"
  2. Pick level — Better quality / Balanced / Smaller file
  3. Compress — Pages rendered to JPEG and rebuilt into new PDF in memory
  4. Download — Save the smaller file; compare with original

Note: This method rebuilds PDF from images. Visual appearance stays, but selectable text, forms, annotations, bookmarks may be lost. Encrypted PDFs show “Compression error”. Large files may be slow.

Best for

  • Scanned documents
  • Image-heavy PDFs
  • Email attachments
  • Upload limits

May be larger for

  • Text-heavy PDFs
  • Already optimized PDFs
  • Vector graphics
  • Try another level or keep original
