How it works

  1. Choose PDF – browse to the file with PDF‑only picker.
  2. Select level – choose quality vs size.
  3. Compress – pages are rendered locally and a new PDF is built in memory.
  4. Download – save your compressed PDF; compare sizes instantly.

Note: Text becomes rasterized, so original searchable text is lost. Works best for scanned or image‑heavy PDFs.

Best for

  • Scanned documents
  • Image‑heavy PDFs
  • Email attachments with large images
  • Restricted upload limits on other tools

May be larger for

  • Pure text PDFs (conversion to images adds size)
  • Optimized PDFs already small
  • Vector graphics and complex drawings

FAQ teaser

Why is my PDF larger after compression? — Rasterizing text increases size for pure‑text PDFs.

Does it keep the original formatting? — Images and layout stay, but text selection and annotations are lost.
