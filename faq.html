FAQ

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. Your PDF is processed locally in your browser — there are no uploads and the extension has no host permissions.

Does it collect data?

No. The only thing stored is an on-device localStorage log with filenames and file sizes; nothing is sent anywhere.

Does it work offline?

Likely yes: a source review on 2026-08-28 found no server upload code and the PDF libraries are bundled in the extension package. However, a real offline browser test is still pending, so we cannot confirm it with certainty yet.

Why is my PDF larger after compression?

Pages are rasterized to JPEG images, and converting a text-heavy or already-optimized PDF to images can add size. The tool works best for scanned or image-heavy PDFs.

Are encrypted PDFs supported?

No. Encrypted PDFs are not supported and produce a generic error.
