Help — SafeCompress PDF

How to Compress

  1. Click icon → Choose file → Select level → Compress → Download
  2. Levels: Low (Better quality), Medium (Balanced), High (Smaller file)

Why output may be larger

Pages become JPEG images. Already-optimized or text-heavy PDFs often grow. Best for scanned/image-heavy PDFs.

Quality

Rasterized — selectable text, forms, annotations, bookmarks lost. JPEG 0.7 lossy — quality drops at High.

Troubleshooting

  • Error “Compression error” — try different PDF, may be encrypted or corrupted
  • Encrypted PDFs not supported
  • Large PDFs may be slow (popup memory)
  • Language follows Chrome setting; theme toggle sun/moon

Logs

LocalStorage log: filename, sizes, export TXT/CSV, clear.
